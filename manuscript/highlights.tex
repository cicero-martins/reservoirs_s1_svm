\begin{itemize}
  \item A/P predicts Sentinel-1 water-area accuracy a priori (from global databases)
  \item Per-scene adaptivity, not polarisation count, drives accuracy
  \item Dual-polarisation helps only at low A/P, where it is also the costliest
  \item A/P selects the cheapest sufficient classifier for each reservoir
  \item An open GEE app applies this A/P selection over 35,000 reservoirs
\end{itemize}
