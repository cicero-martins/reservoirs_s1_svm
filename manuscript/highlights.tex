Shoreline compactness predicts Sentinel-1 water-area monitoring reliability
across reservoirs worldwide\\[0.2em]
Cicero Martins Jr., Fulvio Capodici, Mauro De Marchis, Giuseppe Ciraolo
\end{center}

\vspace{0.8cm}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Shoreline area-to-perimeter ratio (A/P) predicts SAR accuracy in advance
  \item Median KGE is 0.93 above 200 m A/P but only 0.48 below 100 m
  \item Climate adds a secondary control on accuracy beyond A/P (p=0.012)
  \item A simple per-scene VV detector matches a costlier dual-pol SVM overall
  \item An open GEE app flags 35,000+ reservoirs by expected reliability
\end{itemize}
