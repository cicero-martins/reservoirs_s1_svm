\begin{abstract}
% Final numbers (Jul 2026, reference-quality-screened global set): A/P->KGE

% GDW_RESERVOIRS_V1_0 verified live via EE = 35,295 (8 Jul 2026).
% Abstract word count: 150/150 (ENVSOFT Guide for Authors limit, exact). See project memory.
The area-to-perimeter ratio (A/P) of a reservoir shoreline may predict, in
advance, how reliably Sentinel-1 monitors surface area, a low-cost basis for
tracking reservoirs beyond altimetry and gauges. We test this
in an Earth Engine pipeline comparing two scene-adaptive detectors grounded in
JRC, a single-polarisation VV Otsu threshold and a dual-polarisation (VV+VH)
Support Vector Machine (SVM), across 62 reference-screened reservoirs on five
continents plus four in Sicily validated against 3~m PlanetScope near-truth.
A/P is a strong predictor (Spearman $\rho = 0.51$, $p = 3\times10^{-5}$), with
median KGE 0.93 above 200~m and 0.48 below 100~m, and the simpler Otsu matches
the dual-band SVM overall. Climate remains a genuine secondary modulator
($p=0.012$), with humid, vegetated reservoirs underperforming at given A/P.
We release it as an interactive, JRC-auto-trained Earth Engine app
that flags 35,000+ global reservoirs by expected reliability, a practical
screening tool for water managers before any image is processed.
\end{abstract}
