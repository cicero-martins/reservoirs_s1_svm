\begin{abstract}
% Final numbers (Jul 2026, reference-quality-screened global set): A/P->KGE

% GDW_RESERVOIRS_V1_0 verified live via EE = 35,295 (8 Jul 2026).
% Abstract word count: 150/150 (ENVSOFT Guide for Authors limit, exact). See project memory.
The area-to-perimeter ratio (A/P) of a reservoir shoreline may predict, in
advance, how reliably Sentinel-1 monitors surface area; we test this in an
Earth Engine pipeline comparing two scene-adaptive detectors
grounded in JRC: a single-polarisation VV Otsu threshold and a
dual-polarisation (VV+VH) Support Vector Machine (SVM). The set spans 62
reference-screened reservoirs on five continents validated against JRC
time-series, plus four Sicilian reservoirs validated against 3~m PlanetScope
near-truth. A/P is a strong predictor (Spearman $\rho = 0.51$,
$p = 3\times10^{-5}$), with median KGE 0.93 above 200~m and 0.48 below
100~m. Otsu wins outright in most reservoirs (38 of 62 vs the SVM's 24); the
SVM's edge concentrates at low A/P, also seen in Sicily. Climate remains a genuine
secondary modulator ($p=0.012$); humid, vegetated reservoirs underperform at
given A/P. We release it as an interactive, JRC-auto-trained Earth Engine app
that also flags its 35,000+ global reservoirs by expected reliability from A/P.
\end{abstract}
