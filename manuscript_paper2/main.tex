\begin{abstract}
% Abstract (~153 words). Numbers from the Jul-2026 analysis; see project memory.
In this study, we test whether reservoir area--elevation--volume (AEV) curves can be
reconstructed from remote sensing alone. We pair Sentinel-1 SAR water area with a
remote water level to reconstruct bathymetry for the exposed band, validated on nine
Sicilian reservoirs against a 2025 echo-sounder survey and official curves. SWOT water
level matched in-situ gauges with a KGE of $0.94$--$0.99$ at seven of nine reservoirs,
recovering a physically consistent record through four documented gauge malfunctions.
The SAR-observable drawdown band alone captured $39$--$92\%$ (mean $71\%$) of each
reservoir's total design-curve volume. Against a directly-surveyed volume loss at
Garcia, the reconstruction captured $76\%$ of it. A PlanetScope optical reconstruction
agreed with the SAR bathymetry to $1.3$--$1.7$~m at medium-to-high A/P. Reliability
scales with the shoreline area-to-perimeter ratio. Together, these results suggest
reservoir storage could be monitored from space alone, an alternative to outdated
design curves. The pipeline is open-source, with an accompanying Streamlit-based
online visualization tool.
\end{abstract}
