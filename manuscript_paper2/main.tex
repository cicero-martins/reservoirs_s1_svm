\begin{abstract}
% DRAFT abstract (~150 words). Numbers from the Jul-2026 analysis; see project memory.
Reservoir storage assessment relies on area--elevation--volume (AEV) curves that,
in many operational settings, still date to construction and no longer reflect
decades of sedimentation. We present a fully remote-sensing method that reconstructs
updated bathymetry and storage for the SAR-observable, drawdown-exposed band of a
reservoir by pairing Sentinel-1 SAR water area with a remote water level, requiring
neither in-situ gauges nor a nadir altimetry track. Waterlines exposed during drawdown
are stacked by water level into a digital elevation model of the emerged basin, and
storage follows by integration. We validate the method on nine Sicilian reservoirs
against a December~2025 echo-sounder survey (RMSE~$2.2$~m) and recent reference
bathymetric curves. Replacing the in-situ gauge with SWOT swath altimetry changes
the reconstructed storage by a mean of $2.2$~\si{Mm^3} (${\approx}5\%$) relative to
field truth, demonstrating gauge-free, track-free volume. An independent PlanetScope
optical reconstruction agrees with the SAR bathymetry to $1.1$--$2.6$~m at
medium-to-high A/P; at one reservoir whose gauge had stalled at a fixed reading for
months, only SWOT recovered the true drawdown. Reliability scales with the shoreline
area-to-perimeter ratio. We release the pipeline as open-source code, with a
downloadable interactive app.
\end{abstract}
