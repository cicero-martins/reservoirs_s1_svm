\section{Study area and data}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Reservoirs}
We study nine reservoirs in Sicily, Italy, spanning a shoreline area-to-perimeter
ratio (A/P) of ${\approx}50$--$240$~m and a range of sizes and dam ages: \emph{Olivo},
\emph{Ancipa}, \emph{Nicoletti}, \emph{Castello}, \emph{Garcia}, \emph{Arancio},
\emph{Rosamarina}, \emph{Poma} and \emph{Pozzillo} (Fig.~\ref{fig:studyarea}). Coverage
is heterogeneous by design rather than by omission: Table~\ref{tab:coverage} makes this
explicit rather than leaving it implicit. \emph{Garcia} has a genuine field survey
(December-2025 echo-sounder) and is our only true ground truth. Six reservoirs ---
\emph{Poma}, \emph{Rosamarina}, \emph{Arancio}, \emph{Castello}, \emph{Olivo} and
\emph{Nicoletti} --- have a recent official area--volume curve from the regional water
authority, our secondary reference. \emph{Ancipa} and \emph{Pozzillo} lack any modern
survey or curve and rely solely on the PlanetScope cross-sensor check
(Section~\ref{sec:xsensor}), which also covers Poma and Rosamarina as a second,
independent line of evidence.

\subsection{Sentinel-1 SAR water area}
Water-surface area time series (2014--2026) are derived from the Sentinel-1 C-band
GRD collection in Google Earth Engine using the scene-adaptive classifier of
\citet{PaperOne} (per-scene VV Otsu, JRC-auto-trained), which yields one area value
per acquisition after outlier removal and smoothing. For the spatial DEM the same
pipeline exports binary water masks (10~m, EPSG:32633).

\subsection{Water level: gauge, DAHITI, SWOT}
Three water-level sources are used. (i) \emph{In-situ gauges} from the regional
civil-protection network (AEGIS), daily, 2022--2026. (ii) \emph{DAHITI}
\citep{Schwatke2015} nadir-altimetry water level. (iii) \emph{SWOT}
\citep{Biancamaria2016} water-surface elevation from the \texttt{SWOT\_L2\_HR\_LakeSP}
Prior-lake product, retrieved through the PO.DAAC Hydrocron API by prior-lake
identifier, 2023-07 onward, quality-screened. We note (Section~\ref{sec:results})
that for these small reservoirs DAHITI is itself SWOT-derived, so the independent
comparison is gauge versus SWOT.

Three of the nine gauges have a documented malfunction window during which the
recorded level is not usable at face value: Rosamarina's sensor froze at two
different flat readings (2024-01-24--2025-04-02 and 2025-07-24--2026-01-29);
Garcia's sensor reads the dry lakebed floor (${\sim}176$~m) rather than the true
water level whenever the reservoir empties below that elevation
(2025-08-06--2026-02-03, confirmed against a real decline to ${\sim}172$~m in SWOT
over the same window); and Nicoletti's sensor was inoperative until late 2025. In
each case we substitute SWOT (Rosamarina, Garcia) or the design curve inverted from
official monthly volumes (Nicoletti, and as a fallback elsewhere) for the affected
dates rather than discarding them --- the water-level source actually used for every
paired date is recorded and reported per reservoir (Section~\ref{sec:results}). This
recurring, independently-confirmed gauge failure mode is itself an argument for
pairing SAR imagery with a gauge-independent altimetry source rather than relying on
in-situ networks alone.

\subsection{Ground truth}
Independent references are of two different kinds. Garcia has a genuine field survey
--- a December-2025 single-beam echo-sounder / GPS survey ($\sim$194{,}000 XYZ points,
EPSG:32633) whose acquisition we control and describe in full (Section~\ref{sec:res_garcia})
--- which we treat as \emph{ground truth}. Poma, Rosamarina (2025), Arancio (2022),
Castello, Olivo and Nicoletti instead have updated centimetric area--volume curves
supplied by the regional water authority: the best available reference for those
reservoirs, but with a surveying methodology not documented to us, so we report them
as a secondary reference rather than ground truth. The design AEV curves --- predating
each reservoir's operational history, not uniformly from the 1960s --- serve as the
pre-existing baseline against which the SAR-observable \emph{band} capacity change is
compared; the corresponding \emph{total} change is not directly measured
(Section~\ref{sec:uncertainty}).

\subsection{PlanetScope optical imagery}
For an independent cross-sensor check we use PlanetScope surface-reflectance imagery
(3~m, four-band) over the four reservoirs it covers (Poma, Rosamarina, Pozzillo,
Ancipa) for 2024--2025. Water is classified per scene by an NDWI threshold selected
per image; the resulting ${\sim}130$ optical water masks feed the same reconstruction
as the SAR masks (Section~\ref{sec:xsensor}). PlanetScope is the only modern
independent reference available for Pozzillo and Ancipa.
