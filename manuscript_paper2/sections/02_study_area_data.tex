\section{Study area and data}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Reservoirs}
We study nine reservoirs in Sicily, Italy, spanning a shoreline area-to-perimeter
ratio (A/P) of ${\approx}50$--$240$~m and a range of sizes and dam ages: \emph{Olivo},
\emph{Ancipa}, \emph{Nicoletti}, \emph{Castello}, \emph{Garcia}, \emph{Arancio},
\emph{Rosamarina}, \emph{Poma} and \emph{Pozzillo} (Fig.~\ref{fig:studyarea}). Coverage
is heterogeneous by design rather than by omission, and Table~\ref{tab:coverage} makes
this explicit rather than leaving it implicit. \emph{Garcia} has a genuine field survey
(December-2025 echo-sounder) and is our only true ground truth. Six reservoirs
(\emph{Poma}, \emph{Rosamarina}, \emph{Arancio}, \emph{Castello}, \emph{Olivo} and
\emph{Nicoletti}) have a recent official area--volume curve from the regional water
authority, our secondary reference. \emph{Ancipa} and \emph{Pozzillo} lack any modern
survey or curve, so for them the original design curve is the only reference available.

\subsection{Sentinel-1 SAR water area}
Water-surface area time series (2014--2026) are derived from the Sentinel-1 C-band GRD
collection in Google Earth Engine using the scene-adaptive classification of
\citet{PaperOne}, one area value per acquisition on a single automatically selected
orbit, with partial swath coverage filled by mosaicking scenes within $\pm6$~days.
Two products follow from this, used for different purposes. The \emph{time series} carries
\citet{PaperOne}'s published post-processing, outlier rejection followed by LOWESS
smoothing \citep{Cleveland1979}, which suppresses per-scene classification noise rather
than real volume change.
The \emph{calibration pairs} that fit the hypsometric curves instead use raw
per-acquisition areas, since each is matched to an individual SWOT overpass and smoothing
would blend neighbouring dates into pairs never actually observed. For the spatial DEM the
same pipeline exports binary water masks (10~m, EPSG:32633).

\subsection{Water level: gauge and SWOT}
Two water-level sources are used. \emph{In-situ gauges} from the regional
civil-protection network (AEGIS) provide a daily record, 2022--2026.
\emph{SWOT} \citep{Biancamaria2016} provides water-surface elevation from the
\texttt{SWOT\_L2\_HR\_LakeSP} Prior-lake product, retrieved through the PO.DAAC
Hydrocron API by prior-lake identifier, 2023-07 onward, quality-screened. The
water-level source actually used for every paired date is recorded and reported per
reservoir (Section~\ref{sec:results}).

\subsection{Ground truth}
Independent references are of two different kinds. Garcia has a genuine field survey, a
December-2025 single-beam echo-sounder / GPS survey ($\sim$194{,}000 XYZ points,
EPSG:32633) whose acquisition we control and describe in full
(Section~\ref{sec:res_garcia}), and we treat it as \emph{ground truth}. Poma, Rosamarina
(2025), Arancio (2022), Castello, Olivo and Nicoletti instead have updated centimetric
area--volume curves supplied by the regional water authority. These are the best
available reference for those reservoirs, but their surveying methodology is not
documented to us, so we report them as a secondary reference rather than ground truth.
The design AEV curves, which predate each reservoir's operational history and are not
uniformly from the 1960s, serve as the pre-existing baseline against which the
SAR-observable \emph{band} capacity change is compared. The corresponding \emph{total}
change is not directly measured (Section~\ref{sec:capmetrics}).
