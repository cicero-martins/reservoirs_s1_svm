\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We presented a fully remote-sensing method that reconstructs updated reservoir
bathymetry and storage by stacking Sentinel-1 SAR waterlines by water level, with the
level supplied remotely. On nine Sicilian reservoirs the method was validated against
a December-2025 echo-sounder survey (RMSE $2.2$~m) and recent official bathymetric
curves. Replacing the in-situ gauge with SWOT swath altimetry changed reconstructed
storage by a mean of $2.2$~\si{Mm^3} (${\approx}5\%$) relative to field truth,
demonstrating gauge-free and, because SWOT observes by swath, track-free volume.
Reconstruction reliability scales with the shoreline area-to-perimeter ratio and with
drawdown amplitude; the SAR estimate of capacity loss is a validated lower bound,
since deep-zone sedimentation is not exposed. The pipeline is released as an
interactive tool. The approach opens gauge-free, survey-free storage monitoring for
the large population of small, ungauged reservoirs that SWOT has, for the first time,
made observable.
